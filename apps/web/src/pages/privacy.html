Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. This page explains what data Toolpad processes, where it is processed, and the choices you have.

Summary

Toolpad does not require an account, and it does not run its own analytics. All tools run entirely in your browser, so the data you type into them is not sent to us. Advertising and fonts are loaded from Google, as described below.

Tools that run in your browser

Data generators, formatters, converters and calculators run in JavaScript on your device. Text, numbers and generated results you enter or see on these pages stay in your browser. This includes the password generator, the JSON and XML formatters, the Base64 and hex encoders, and the EMI, GST and SIP calculators.

Local storage in your browser

Toolpad stores two preferences in your browser's local storage: your light or dark theme choice (toolpad-theme) and a short list of recently used tools (toolpad-recent). This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Cookies and advertising

Toolpad's own code does not set cookies. We show advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, including ads based on your previous visits to this site and others. You can review and change ad personalisation in Google's ad settings, and read more in how Google uses data from partner sites.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the site loads its fonts from Google's servers, so your browser sends a request to Google that includes your IP address.
  • Google AdSense: loads and shows advertising, as described above.
  • Product Hunt: the footer badge is an image loaded from Product Hunt's servers.

Your choices

  • Use most tools without submitting anything: they work without sending data to us.
  • Clear the toolpad-theme and toolpad-recent entries from your browser's local storage.
  • Manage ad personalisation in Google's ad settings, or use a browser setting or extension that blocks advertising.
