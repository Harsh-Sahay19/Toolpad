What this address generator does

The generator produces postal addresses for the United States, India and the United Kingdom, formatted the way each country writes them. Each address has a street or locality, a town or city, a region, and a postal code in a valid range for that region. The addresses are for testing forms, checkout flows, databases and demos.

How to use it

  1. Choose a country with the selector.
  2. Choose how many addresses you want (4, 8 or 12).
  3. Click Generate new addresses. You can also copy one address with its own Copy button, copy all of them, or download the CSV.

How each country's format works

  • United States: house number, street name and type, city, two-letter state code and a five-digit ZIP code.
  • India: flat or house number, locality, city, state and a six-digit PIN code. The first digits of each PIN match the real prefix for its city or state.
  • United Kingdom: house number and street, then town and a postcode made of an outward and an inward part.

The postal ranges are realistic, but the street names, house numbers and localities are generated. A generated address does not point to a real property, and it will not resolve on a map.

Limits and responsible use

  • Use these addresses for form validation, database seeding and mock-ups. They are not suitable for deliveries, verification or identity checks.
  • Do not use a generated address to impersonate a real person or to register for a service in someone else's name.
  • Postal formats change over time. Check formats against your country's postal authority before you rely on them for production validation.
